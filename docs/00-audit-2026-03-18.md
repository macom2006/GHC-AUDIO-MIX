## What is already right

Credit where it is due — several things in this file are properly engineered:

- **48 kHz, internal clock.** Correct, and correct for video.
- **PA is split L / C / R across Matrix 1, 2, 3**, each with its own delay
  (13.1 ms / 10.1 ms / 7.1 ms) and its own 31-band GEQ (FX12 / FX13 / FX14).
  That is real alignment work, properly done.
- **Main 1 has a limiter** (FX8) protecting the PA, plus a bus compressor
  (3:1, −18 dB, 20 ms attack).
- **Crowd mics exist** (ch 29 / 30, A-23 / A-24, phantom on) and are correctly
  **unassigned from Main 1** — they cannot feed back into the house.
- **Every channel has a high-pass filter**, nearly all at 24 dB/oct. This is the
  single most common thing churches skip, and you have not skipped it.
- **A full broadcast mastering chain is already built** on Matrix 5 "STREAM":
  Pulsar EQ, ECL33 compressor/limiter, TAPE pre-insert (FX15), LIMITER post-insert
  (FX16), +5.3 dB input trim.
- **A 40-channel USB split to the DAW is patched** (USB out 1–40, taken straight
  from the stage box inputs, so pre-processing — exactly the right tap point).
- **Vocals are consistently processed**: HPF ~185 Hz, gentle expander, 4:1
  compressor. A coherent approach applied across all of them.

Somebody who knew what they were doing built a lot of this. The problems below are
mostly **things that were started and never finished**, plus one category of
housekeeping that has never been done at all.

## CRITICAL — fix before the next service

### C1. There are no scene safes. None at all.

`ce_data.safes` is completely empty — no channel safes, no preamp safes, no output
safes, no DCA, mute, main or matrix safes.

**What this means:** any scene or snapshot recall overwrites **everything**,
including every preamp gain and every monitor send. Recall a scene mid-service and
you can change the pastor's gain, blow up a musician's in-ear mix, and re-patch
outputs in one button press.

This is the highest live-operational risk in the file. Fix it first.

### C2. Bus 7 "BROADCAST" is muted

Bus 7 is named BROADCAST, sits at unity, and receives sends from:

- Bass (−3.0), E Gtr (−6.0), AC Gtr (−6.0), Keys 1 (−4.0), Keys 2 (−4.0)
- Vox 1–4 (0.0 each), Pastor Lapel (0.0), Raissa, House, Wireless 3, Jude (0.0),
  Brenda (−3.0), Wireless 4 (−6.0), Sax (−6.0), LOOP (−6.0)
- Ch 33 (−10.0), PC Playback (−6.0)
- Bus 13 Choir (−3.0), Bus 14 Inst Verb (−3.0), Bus 15 Vox Verb (−3.0),
  Bus 16 Slap (−3.0)

It feeds **Matrix 5 STREAM at 0 dB**. Somebody built a genuine broadcast mix here.

**It is muted.** Everything above is dead.

### C3. Matrix 5 "STREAM" is not patched to any output

Searching the entire output patch — local XLR, AUX, AES50 A/B/C, StageConnect, USB,
card, MADI, REC, AES/EBU — **Matrix 5's legs (MTX.9 / MTX.10) appear nowhere.**

The broadcast mastering chain described above is processing audio and sending it to
no destination.

### C4. What the USB outputs actually carry is wrong

| USB out | Input-side label | Actually sourced from | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 43 | "FULL MIX R" | **Bus 4 L** | Bus 4 fader is **−52.3 dB** |
| 44 | "FULL MIX R" | **Bus 4 L** (same leg twice) | Same |
| 47 | — | **Main 4 L** | Main 4 is **muted, −144 dB** |
| 48 | — | **Main 4 L** (same leg twice) | Same |

Whatever is meant to be a "full mix" on USB 43/44 is a near-silent bus, in mono,
duplicated to both legs. USB 47/48 are a muted main, also duplicated.

**This is the question I need you to answer:** how does the Osee actually get audio
today? The candidates are:

1. **Analog from LCL 6 / 7 / 8** — but those are PA C, PA L and PA R, so the stream
   would be getting a house PA leg, not a broadcast mix.
2. **From the MADI card (MOD).** MOD outputs carry direct splits of stage inputs
   A-1 … A-32 to something external, and MOD **inputs** are named "AL FOH" (MOD-1/2)
   and "AL BCAST" (MOD-3). That suggests a separate broadcast system downstream. If
   so, the WING's own BROADCAST bus and STREAM matrix are half-built leftovers, and
   the real mix lives on another desk.
3. **USB into a computer** — in which case it is currently receiving near-silence.

Note that ch 35 ("AL FOH", MOD-1) sits at −69.7 dB routed nowhere, and ch 36
("AL BCAST", MOD-3) is at −144 routed nowhere, so the returns from that external
system are not being used either.

Tell me which of the three it is and I will finish the routing properly.

### C5. Subwoofers are fed the full house mix

Matrix 4 "SUBS" (mono, no delay) is fed from **Main 1 at 0 dB**. Nothing else feeds
it. That means every vocal, guitar, cymbal, hi-hat and spoken word is dumping
low-frequency energy into the subs.

This is almost certainly the largest single audible improvement available to you in
the room. See R5 in the remediation plan.

## HIGH

### H1. The stream level follows the house master

`Main 1 → MX5` is **post-fader** (`pre: false`). Pull the house master down for a
quiet moment and the stream goes down with it. The online mix must be independent
of the house master.

### H2. No lip-sync delay

Matrix 5 STREAM has delay **off**. There is no audio delay compensating for video
processing latency anywhere in the file. (Main 1 has a delay value of 40.5 ms
stored but **disabled** — a leftover.)

### H3. Automix is off

`cfg.amix` = `{x: false, y: false}`. You have at minimum nine speech-capable
channels live simultaneously (Pastor Lapel, House, Raissa, Jude, Brenda,
Wireless 3, Wireless 4, Lead 2, Kids Lapel). Running them all open costs
gain-before-feedback and adds open-mic noise to both the room and the stream.

### H4. No mute groups exist

All eight mute groups are unnamed and unused (`MGRP.1` … `MGRP.8`).

The consequence that matters most: **there is no one-button mute for the crowd
mics.** During an altar call, private prayer or pastoral ministry, muting ch 29 and
ch 30 requires finding two channels on a layer. That is a privacy exposure on a
permanent public recording.

### H5. There is no speech DCA

Your DCAs are: Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Horns, BGVS, Effects, PC, BAND.
DCA 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16 are unused; DCA 12 is named "DELAY" and sits at −∞.

**The pastor and all eight wireless channels have no DCA.** The most important
fader in the building during the sermon does not exist. Everything has to be ridden
on individual channels.

### H6. Talkback is assigned but goes nowhere

`cfg.talk.assign = CH40`, but every destination in both talkback groups A and B is
`false`. No bus, no matrix, no main. Talkback produces nothing.

### H7. Virtual soundcheck is not configured

The USB **input** ports are carefully named — KICK, SNARE, HI HAT, RACK 1, RACK 2,
FLOOR TOM, BASS, E GTR, KEYS 1 L, KEYS 2 L, AC GTR, PASTOR LAPEL, VOX 1–4, CRWD 2,
FULL MIX — so someone clearly intended this. But:

- `io.altsw` = false
- **No channel has an alt source pointing at USB.** The only alt source set in the
  entire file is on **ch 12 (Keys 2)**, pointing at **A-14** (Keys 2 R) — which is
  not a USB return and is almost certainly a mis-click.

The groundwork is done. The feature is not switched on.

### H8. Mains 2, 3 and 4 are unused

Every one of the 40 channels is assigned to **Main 1 only**. Mains 2, 3, 4 are
muted at −144.

The WING's defining feature is four stereo main buses with an independent send
level **and pan** per channel. You are running broadcast off a mix bus instead,
which costs you a bus and gives you no independent pan. Main 2 is the right home
for the broadcast mix.

## LOW — tuning observations

These are judgement calls, not errors. Listed so they are on the record.

| # | Observation |
|---|---|
| L1 | **Kick gate release is 400 ms** with 20 ms hold. Long — likely to hold open through fast passages. 150–250 ms is more typical. |
| L2 | **Kick EQ is very scooped**: −14.7 dB at 295 Hz (Q 1.36) and −15.0 dB at 900 Hz (Q 3.24), with +7.8 dB at 7.3 kHz. Defensible as a modern kick sound, but that is a lot of subtraction. Worth an A/B. |
| L3 | **Vocal and speech compressors use a 30 ms attack** at 4:1, threshold −37. Fine for singing; slightly loose for speech, where 5–10 ms holds the level more consistently. The Pastor Lapel channel uses the same 30 ms. |
| L4 | **Ch 8 Bass has the 76LA compressor in the gate slot** (ratio 8) and an expander in the dynamics slot. This works — WING allows it — but it is the opposite of where the next operator will look. |
| L5 | **Main 1 has a 40.5 ms delay stored but disabled.** Harmless, but it will confuse someone eventually. |
| L6 | **WING-LIVE SD recorder is set to 32 tracks** on both slots, and the 2-track recorder to 24-bit stereo off Main 1 L/R. Correct, though 32 tracks does not capture all 40 channels. |
| L7 | **Ch 2 "Snare" LPF is at 20 kHz** — on, but doing nothing. Either set it meaningfully (~12 kHz) or switch it off. |
